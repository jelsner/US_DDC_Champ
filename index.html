Player registration is open

Register below for the 2026 US DDC Championship. Check back for schedule, lodging, field location, and participant updates.

Registration

Register for the 2026 US DDC Championship. Each player registers individually at $20 per person ($40 per team of two). You’ll provide your name, your partner’s name, and your division — then pay by Venmo.

Entry fee

$20 per person. Both players on a team should register and pay separately. Include your name in the payment note so we can match your entry.

Payment

After submitting the registration form, send your $20 entry fee via Venmo:

Venmo @James-Elsner-2

Divisions

  • Open — any team of two players
  • Women’s — teams of two women

What to include

  • Your name
  • Your email (recommended)
  • Partner name
  • Division: Open or Women’s
